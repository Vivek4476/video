Questions

Before you reply

What do you need from me?

A link to the product, a login or a short screen recording, and who the video is for. If you have brand colours and fonts, send those too. I write the script.

Do you do voice-over?

Yes. Either a natural AI voice included in the price, or your own recording, which I clean up and sync. Videos also work silent, with captions.

How many revisions?

Two rounds on the first cut. Changes to the script are free until you sign off the storyboard.

How does payment work?

Half upfront, half on delivery, by PayPal, Wise or bank transfer. Fixed price agreed in writing before any work starts.

Who owns the video?

You do, fully, once the final invoice is paid. Music and footage are licensed for commercial use and listed with the delivery.
