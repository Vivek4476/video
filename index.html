Questions

Have you done this for clients before?

Not yet. The films above are self-initiated. That's why the script and storyboard come first, for a $100 deposit, and you can stop there.

What do you need from me?

A link to your product, a login or a short screen recording, and who the video is for. Send your brand colors and fonts if you have them. I write the script.

What if I don't like it?

If you don't like the script and storyboard, we stop and you've paid $100. After that, you get two full rounds of changes on the first cut.

Do you do voice-over?

Yes. A natural AI voice is included, or send your own recording and I'll clean it up and sync it. Videos also work muted, with captions.

How does payment work?

$100 deposit for the script and storyboard. The rest on delivery, by PayPal, Wise or bank transfer.

Who owns the video?

You do, once it's paid for. Music and footage are licensed for commercial use, and I list every license with the delivery.
